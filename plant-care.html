Plant Care Guide

Learn how to keep your plants fresh and healthy

Take Care of Your Green Friends

Plants need proper care to grow well. Simple activities like watering, providing sunlight and using suitable soil can help plants stay healthy.

Essential Plant Care Tips

Watering

Water plants according to their needs. Avoid giving too much water.

Sunlight

Place plants where they receive the suitable amount of sunlight.

Healthy Soil

Use suitable soil that provides nutrients and proper drainage.

Pruning

Remove dry leaves and damaged parts to maintain plant health.

Simple Tips for Beginners

  • Choose plants suitable for your location.
  • Check the soil before watering.
  • Use pots with drainage holes.
  • Remove dry and damaged leaves.
  • Observe your plants regularly.
